06Questions

Questions

How is pricing set?

A flat monthly price, quoted after the free assessment. It depends on how many people and offices you have. Nonprofits get lower rates.

Do we have to replace our current tools?

No. We start with what you have. If something is too old to be safe, we tell you and show you the cost, and you decide when to replace it.

Can you work with our in-house IT person?

Yes. We can take the parts they don't have time for, like security and backups, and cover for them when they're away.

Are you really available 24/7?

Yes. Urgent problems, like an office that's offline or an account that's been hacked, get a response at any hour, every day.

Where is your team?

In Minnesota, and all of our support happens in the US.

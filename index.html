Services

Everything your staff log in to, looked after.

One flat monthly price covers day-to-day help and the security work. The same two people do both, so whoever fixes a printer also knows who should have access to what.

  • Managed IT and helpdesk

    When something stops someone's work, they email us or message us in Teams, and one of us picks it up. Most fixes happen over a remote session. We already know their laptop, mailbox and office network, so nobody has to explain it all again.

    • Password resets and lockouts
    • Onboarding and offboarding
    • Printers, scanners and shared drives
    • Software installs and updates

  • Cybersecurity

    Most attacks start with a fake email or a stolen password. These fake emails (phishing) often look like an invoice or a shared file. We lock down accounts, email and devices, watch for signs of trouble, and step in right away if something gets through.

    • MFA: a second check at every sign-in
    • Conditional access: blocks risky sign-ins, where licensed
    • EDR: spots and stops attacks on each device
    • Email filtering
    • SPF, DKIM and DMARC: stop fake mail from your domain
    • Updates installed on time
    • Help if you're hacked
    • Security training for staff

  • Microsoft 365 and Teams

    We set up your Microsoft 365 account so it fits how your staff work, and check that you pay for the right licenses and nothing extra. Some protections on this page, like conditional access and Intune, need Business Premium or an add-on. We show you that cost before we suggest it. If you use Google Workspace, we look after that instead.

    • Entra ID: staff accounts and sign-in rules
    • License review, with nonprofit discounts
    • Email setup
    • Teams and SharePoint folders and permissions
    • Teams Phone and call routing

  • Networking and multi-site

    Staff get the same files and the same Teams at every office. We split the network so guests can't reach staff computers or printers, and every site connects through a firewall we manage. We can usually keep the equipment you already have.

    • Firewalls
    • Office Wi-Fi, with guest Wi-Fi kept separate
    • VLANs: separate networks on the same equipment
    • Site-to-site VPN: a private link between offices
    • Safe access from home

  • Backup and recovery

    A backup only counts if it restores, so we test yours. Phones need to keep ringing too, so we set up call routing that keeps your main line working if one office loses its internet.

    • Email and file backups a hacked account can't delete
    • Test restores on a schedule
    • A written plan for the day an office goes offline

  • Devices

    We set up laptops and phones the same way every time and keep them updated. Where your licenses include it, we manage them with Intune, Microsoft's device tool. If one goes missing, we can lock or wipe it from here.

    • Laptop and phone setup
    • Disk encryption and screen locks
    • Remote lock and wipe
    • System and app updates
    • Work data kept separate on personal phones

Security

Security is part of the monthly work.

It's in the same agreement as the helpdesk, and the same two people do it. Here is the basic setup we put in place for every client. During the assessment we check it against what you have now.

  • Sign-insMFA on every account, admins first. That's a second check, like a code on your phone. Where your licenses allow, we also block risky sign-ins.
  • DevicesEDR on every computer. It watches for attacks and stops them. Each device also gets updates and disk encryption.
  • EmailFilters catch phishing and malware. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are settings that stop strangers from sending email as you.
  • NetworkFirewalls we manage, guest Wi-Fi kept apart from staff, and a safe way in for people working from home.
  • BackupsCopies a hacked account can't delete. We restore from them on a schedule to prove they work.
  • PeopleShort security training for staff, and hands-on help if something gets through.

When your records are sensitive

Plenty of organizations hold information that would hurt someone if it got out: client files, health details, finances, home addresses. For some, a single exposed address or a forwarded email can put a person in danger. If that's you, we set things up with that in mind.

  • Locations stay private

    If any of your addresses are confidential, we keep them out of email signatures, shared calendars and device location settings. We also check what photos, Wi-Fi names and online listings give away, and help you get them taken down.

  • Your data stays yours

    We don't open client files or mailboxes. Our everyday accounts can't reach them, we use separate admin accounts only when a task needs one, and admin activity is logged in your own Microsoft 365 account, where you can see it. If a fix ever brings us near client data, we ask first. Your organization owns its data, and we put that in writing.

  • Least access, every login protected

    Staff see the records their role needs and nothing more. Every account gets MFA, admins included, and conditional access wherever your licenses include it. Nobody shares a login.

  • Support without client details

    A support request never needs a client's name or address. We train staff to describe the problem, not the person. If client information lands in a support email by mistake, we delete it and tell you.

  • Built around your rules

    Many organizations work under rules about who can see what: HIPAA for health information, confidentiality duties for legal and financial clients, and VAWA and FVPSA for survivor services. We set up your tools to fit the rules you work under and keep our own access as narrow as the work allows.

  • Staff safety counts too

    When someone on your staff is being harassed or targeted online, we help lock down their accounts and devices. Then we walk them through what was exposed and what to change.

During the assessment we check each of these against your setup and show you what we found. Questions before then? Email us.

How it works

The first ninety days, written down before we start.

It starts with a free assessment. We visit, look at your network, Microsoft 365, devices and backups, and give you a written summary whether or not you hire us. If you do, this is the plan.

  1. Days 1–30

    Learn your setup, fix what's urgent

    • Walk every site and meet the staff
    • Learn the tools and equipment you already use

  2. Days 31–60

    Secure the basics

    • Remove accounts for people who have left

  3. Days 61–90

    Plan the next year

    • A written roadmap in plain English
    • Costs laid out to fit your budget year
    • Your first quarterly review with leadership

Before day one

FAQ

Common questions.

What does the free assessment involve?

We start with a call, then visit your main office and any others where it makes sense. We look at your network and firewall, Microsoft 365 setup, devices, backups and security settings, and we talk with whoever handles IT today. You get a written summary of what we found and what we'd fix first. It's yours to keep whether or not you hire us.

How is pricing set?

Flat monthly, quoted after the assessment, with lower rates for nonprofits. The number depends on how many people and sites you have, not on how many times you contact us. The quote lists the security and backup tools we use and whether each one is included in the monthly price or billed at cost, so it can go into a budget, or a grant, line by line. It also says what would cost extra, like new hardware or a large project.

What are your hours, and what happens after hours?

Regular support runs on weekday business hours, with the exact hours set in your agreement. Outside them we keep an urgent route for a short list of emergencies: phones or internet down at an office, an account that looks compromised, a lost device with sensitive data on it, or signs of ransomware. Those reach both of us at any hour, and the callback goal goes in writing before you sign. Password resets and printer trouble wait until morning. We won't promise a round-the-clock helpdesk that two people can't honestly staff.

Do you only work in Minnesota?

We're based in Sherburne County, northwest of the Twin Cities, and most day-to-day support happens remotely. When something can't be fixed from here, one of us comes to the office. During the assessment we agree how quickly we can reach each of your locations and put it in writing.

Do we have to replace our current tools or hardware?

No. We start with what you have, whether that's Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and whatever computers and network gear are in the office. If something is too old to get security updates or puts you at risk, we tell you and show you the cost. You decide when to replace it.

We have an in-house IT person. Can you work with them?

Yes. We split the work in the way that suits you. For example, they handle day-to-day requests and we handle security, backups and the network. We share documentation and access so either side can step in.

Can you work alongside our current provider during a handover?

Yes. We work in parallel for an agreed period, collect admin access and documentation properly, and take over once nothing is left loose. Switching to us lists what we collect.

Do you help with Microsoft 365 licensing?

Yes, for businesses and nonprofits alike. For nonprofits: Microsoft ended its free Business Premium and Office 365 E1 grants for nonprofits, starting at each organization's first renewal after July 1, 2025. Business Basic is still free for up to 300 users, and the paid plans carry nonprofit discounts. Some protections on this page, like conditional access and Intune, need Business Premium or an add-on, and we'll show you the cost before suggesting it. We check what you have, what you pay for and what your staff actually use, then suggest the smallest setup that covers your security needs.

What happens if one of you is out sick?

The other one covers. We both work in your environment, keep shared documentation of your setup, and hold the access needed to step in. If one of us will be away for a planned stretch, you'll hear about it ahead of time.

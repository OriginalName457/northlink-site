06Questions

Common questions

What does it cost?

Every plan is built around what you run. After the free assessment you get a clear quote that covers what you use and nothing you don't. If you're paying for IT that isn't delivering, the assessment will show you where.

Do we have to replace our current tools?

No. We work with the tools you already use, whatever they are. If something is too old to be safe, we tell you and show you the cost, and you decide when to replace it.

We're starting from scratch. Can you set everything up?

Yes. We set up your accounts, email, devices, network and security from day one, built around how your team works, then support it from there.

Can you work with our in-house IT person?

Yes. We take the parts they don't have time for, like security and backups, and cover for them when they're away.

Is support available 24/7?

Yes. Urgent problems, like an office that's offline or an account that's been hacked, get a response at any hour, every day.

Where is your team?

In Minnesota, and all of our support happens in the US.
